Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

GymTap - One Tap Check-In ("GymTap", "we", "our", or "us") is a minimal gym habit tracking app provided by LS Commerce GmbH.

Summary

GymTap is designed to keep your workout habit data on your own devices. We do not sell your data, we do not use third-party advertising, and we do not use third-party analytics in the app.

Data You Enter in the App

GymTap may store information you enter or create in the app, such as check-in days, workout schedule preferences, onboarding preferences, dark mode settings, streak-related data, and optional macro or nutrition entries if you use those features.

Where Data Is Stored

Your GymTap data is stored locally on your iPhone and, when you use the Apple Watch app, may be synced to your paired Apple Watch through Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. We do not operate a GymTap account system or developer server that receives this app data.

Apple Services

Apple may process information related to App Store downloads, TestFlight, purchases, crash reports, diagnostics, and device operation according to Apple's own policies and your device settings. We do not control Apple's processing of that information.

Third Parties

GymTap does not include third-party advertising SDKs or third-party analytics SDKs.

Tracking

GymTap does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

Children's Privacy

GymTap is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting Your Data

Because GymTap stores app data locally, you can delete your GymTap data by deleting the app from your iPhone and Apple Watch. If the app adds account, cloud sync, or server features in the future, this policy will be updated before those features are released.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when GymTap changes. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
