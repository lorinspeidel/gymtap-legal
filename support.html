Apple Watch

If the Apple Watch app does not appear, confirm that GymTap is installed on your iPhone, your Apple Watch is paired and unlocked, and automatic app install is enabled in the Watch app on iPhone. You can also open the Watch app on iPhone, find GymTap, and install it manually if it is listed.

Sync

GymTap syncs check-in information between iPhone and Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. If sync seems delayed, open GymTap on both devices while they are near each other.

Data

GymTap stores your app data locally on your devices. See the Privacy Policy and Data and Privacy Choices pages for more information.
