Data and Privacy Choices

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Delete Local App Data

GymTap stores habit, check-in, preference, and optional macro data locally on your devices. To delete that local data, delete GymTap from your iPhone and Apple Watch.

Request Help

If you have questions about privacy or data deletion, contact lorin.speidel@gmail.com.

No Account Data

GymTap does not currently use a GymTap account system or developer server, so we do not have a server-side account profile to delete.
